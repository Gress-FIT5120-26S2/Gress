Privacy Policy

Version / effective date: 2026-10-12

Who operates KitchMemo

KitchMemo is operated by Haoxuan Sun. Contact nariyirm@gmail.com for privacy requests, questions or complaints. Effective date: 12 October 2026.

Your device identity

KitchMemo creates a pseudonymous device identity and secret credential so your fridge and personal learning records can be restored between sessions. A nickname is optional. There is no email/password login. A recovery code can transfer your identity to another installation; keep it private.

Information we process

We process your installation identifier, nickname, authentication and recovery records; fridge membership and invitations; food names, quantities, storage, dates, prices, notes, shopping lists and food outcomes; learning activity, quiz answers, progress and achievements; AI conversations, feedback and preferences; and notification settings and push tokens. We also process request/security information such as IP address, timestamps and errors through our hosting providers. We do not request your contacts, precise location or microphone. No advertising or cross-app tracking SDK is included.

Purpose and household sharing

This information provides inventory management, reminders, shared shopping, learning, optional AI assistance, security and support. Members of the same shared fridge can see household inventory, shopping activity, your nickname and household achievements. Personal learning and private AI conversations are not shared with other fridge members. Joining a shared fridge does not make your personal chat history public. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Optional AI and photo processing

Before an AI feature sends data to external processors, the app asks for permission. OpenAI processes assistant messages, relevant conversation history, course context and authorized fridge information. Google Gemini processes food/product names and units to suggest food or packaging metadata. Cloudflare Workers AI creates icons from food/category names or fixed material prompts; generated food reference icons may be stored publicly. The food-photo recognition service at d12q94a0v4ih7q.cloudfront.net receives only the photo you choose to upload, its filename and request metadata; AWS CloudFront provides its delivery infrastructure. It is a separate recognition service, not OpenAI or Gemini photo processing. Do not upload faces, personal documents or confidential text. The app does not automatically upload your photo library.

Your AI choice

You can decline the prompt or revoke AI permission under Profile → Privacy. Manual inventory, shopping and course activities remain available. Revocation stops future AI requests; it does not recall data already processed by a provider or erase prior chat history. Permission is tied to the installation and must be granted again after identity deletion or on another installation. AI suggestions may be inaccurate. Photos and estimates cannot establish food safety; follow product use-by labels and official guidance.

Service providers and overseas processing

Supabase hosts our database, storage and realtime invalidation service. Vercel hosts the API and public pages. Expo and Apple/Google deliver optional notifications. A notification may include a food name or household activity, so check your lock-screen settings. Open Food Facts receives barcode lookups for product information. The AI providers listed above process optional AI requests. These services may process information outside Australia. Provider processing and security retention are subject to their own terms and policies. We request OpenAI responses with store disabled; this does not mean all provider security logging is disabled.

Retention

Inventory and learning records remain while your device identity is active or until you delete them. Private assistant/tutor conversations have a 30-day access window; expired records may remain until cleanup. Tutor cleanup runs daily, with request/intervention records retained for up to 90 days. Photos are proxied in server memory and are not deliberately saved to our database or photo storage; external recognition processing may have separate retention. Hosting/security logs and provider backups follow their configured retention. We do not promise that a deletion instantly removes provider backups, logs or data already received by a third party.

Delete your identity and data

Open Profile → Privacy → Delete my data, read the explanation and confirm. No email or support call is required. This removes your device profile, credentials, recovery code, push tokens, personal learning and private chats, plus inventory and shopping entries you added or own. Your custom categories are removed; other members’ items in those categories move to Other. Shared containers and other members’ records remain, with your creator/actor links removed; anonymous household totals may remain. A shared fridge you created returns to a generic name. The service retains only an irreversible fingerprint of the erased installation ID to prevent its old credentials from recreating the identity. Generated category assets are removed from storage, with failed removals queued for retry. Existing push notifications already delivered to another device cannot be recalled.

Permissions, requests and changes

Camera and photo-library access is used only when you choose photo capture or selection. Notification permission is optional and can be changed in system settings. You can edit your nickname, manage recovery and change privacy choices in the app. For access, correction, unresolved deletion, provider questions or a privacy complaint, email nariyirm@gmail.com; do not email credentials or recovery codes. We will assess and respond to your request, and may need to verify your control of the installation. KitchMemo is not designed specifically for children. Material changes will update this page; changes to AI processing will require renewed permission where appropriate.
