Why KitchMemo?

Forgotten food. Repeat purchases. Everyday effort.

Young Australian households can lose track of what is at home, what needs using, and what to buy next.

Our starting point is simple: make food easier to remember. KitchMemo connects inventory, freshness, shopping and learning so households can make confident decisions and waste less together.

01   Capture

Add food through photo-assisted, barcode or manual entry.

02   Know

03   Act

Use food in time, plan shopping and learn practical actions.
