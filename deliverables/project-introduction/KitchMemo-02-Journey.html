ITERATION 2

Turn knowledge into action

Fridge-aware guidance and positive feedback for everyday food habits.

Spoonie AI

“What should I use first?”

Ask freely or use quick prompts. Spoonie grounds its guidance in your household fridge, helping you review food and plan restocking.

Inventory changes only happen after you confirm. Requests outside its purpose are safely declined.

Kitchen Wins

Save food. Earn XP. Climb higher.

Levels, a medal wall and daily / weekly quests reward real food actions, encouraging better habits through positive feedback.

Also improved: barcode entry, shopping and restocking, fridge filters, and inventory outcome tracking.
