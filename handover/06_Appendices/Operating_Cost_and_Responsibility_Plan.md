# KitchMemo Operating Cost and Responsibility Plan

This plan gives a receiving sponsor a practical basis for budgeting and assigning service duties. It contains no invented subscription tiers, account balances, prices or staffing commitments. Actual costs depend on privately confirmed provider plans, workload and regions.

## Cost drivers to verify

| Service or work | Cost driver | Receiving decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supabase | Database compute/storage, egress, Realtime, Storage and backup/restore options | Confirm current plan, data growth, backup retention and budget owner |
| Vercel | Function usage/duration, traffic, observability and deployment plan | Confirm runtime limits, alerts and production project billing |
| Expo EAS | Cloud builds, distribution/supporting services and build quota | Confirm team plan and future release frequency |
| Apple distribution | Developer account and signing/distribution ownership | Confirm renewal custody and transfer requirements |
| OpenAI | Assistant/tutor generation, embeddings and evaluations | Set usage budgets; retain idempotency, bounds and optional-feature disablement |
| Gemini | Food/packaging enrichment | Confirm request quotas, retention terms and manual fallback |
| Cloudflare | AI icon generation and associated account services | Prefer cached/reference assets and monitor backfill costs |
| Photo model/AWS | Model hosting, CloudFront traffic and infrastructure | Obtain separate service ownership, hosting costs and reproducible deployment |
| Support and content review | Staff time, independent source review and UAT | Assign people and scope instead of assuming the student team continues indefinitely |
| Backup and continuity | Protected database/Storage copies and restore drill | Fund tested protection rather than relying on a user recovery code |

## Proposed operational responsibilities

| Role | Decisions and routine responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Product owner | Scope, accepted risk, user feedback, backlog priority and pilot evaluation |
| Support lead | Public contact, redacted incident intake, user training and escalation |
| Technical maintainer | Compatible source/dependency changes, API/native releases and troubleshooting |
| Database/security owner | Migration approval, privileged access, retention, backup/restore and erasure monitoring |
| Content reviewer | Food/disposal sources, course answers, tutor evidence and independent review |
| Account/budget owner | Subscriptions, permissions, renewal, provider limits and cost alerts |
| Portfolio/submission owner | PG organisation, course requirements, links and actual signatures |

These are functions to assign, not claims about individual team duties. One person can cover multiple roles if competent and authorised. Use the Access and Ownership Register to record actual appointments.

## First operating month

During takeover, verify installation/access, UAT, the actual support channel and backup plan. During the first week observe real cron/push outcomes, provider usage and emerging failures. During the first month complete the isolated restore drill and independent educational review, resolve the known visual/testing issues and agree measured pilot objectives. Timing is proposed and should be adjusted to the sponsor's risk acceptance and capacity.

No team post-handover support SLA, commercial warranty or recurring budget is established by this package. Any arrangement should name its owner, duration, deliverables and escalation terms before acceptance.
