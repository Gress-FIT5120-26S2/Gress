# KitchMemo Product Direction

## Register

product

## Target Users

KitchMemo primarily serves people aged 18–35 who manage food in a young household. They may live alone or with a partner and are generally not sharing the home with older family members. They want practical help with ingredient freshness, everyday meal decisions, and fridge organisation without the experience feeling like household administration.

## Product Purpose

KitchMemo connects fridge ingredient management with intelligent recipe discovery. The home screen should quietly answer the most urgent everyday question—“Which ingredients are close to expiring?”—while keeping the interactive 3D kitchen as the product’s main experience and avoiding premature exposure of detailed management tools.

## Brand Personality

- Quiet and intelligent
- Warm and lived-in
- Gently playful through the miniature kitchen world
- Restrained in hierarchy and natural in motion
- Helpful without creating anxiety

## Anti-references

- Alarm-heavy red warnings or fear-based freshness messaging
- Dense inventory dashboards and analytics panels
- Stacks of generic cards on the home screen
- Full ingredient lists or detailed recipe feeds before the user asks for them
- Text-heavy controls that compete with the 3D kitchen
- Aggressive gamification or productivity-style progress metrics

## Design Principles

1. **Keep the kitchen as the hero.** Supporting information should frame the 3D scene, not cover it.
2. **Reveal one useful fact at a time.** The home screen should surface the highest-priority household signal and defer detail to the relevant destination page.
3. **Prefer ambient state over dashboard UI.** Use light, colour, motion, and in-scene indicators to express freshness or time when they remain understandable.
4. **Make spatial interactions feel direct.** Camera movement, object animation, and page transitions should form one continuous response to a tap.
5. **Use calm urgency.** Near-expiry states should be noticeable through warm amber accents and clear language, reserving red for genuinely critical states.
6. **Preserve visual breathing room.** Empty space is intentional when it strengthens focus and atmosphere.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

- Maintain WCAG AA-level contrast for essential text and controls.
- Support system text scaling without hiding essential information.
- Provide reduced-motion alternatives for camera and transition animations.
- Never communicate freshness state through colour alone.
- Keep interactive targets comfortably tappable and provide accessible labels for 3D hotspots.
